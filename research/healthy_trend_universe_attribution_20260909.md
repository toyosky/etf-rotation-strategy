# Healthy Trend Universe Attribution — preregistration

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: preregistered, results pending

## Question
Why do apparently healthy OLS trends have materially different forward returns in the curated fixed15 universe versus Cluster90?

This is a mechanism/attribution diagnostic only. It does not introduce a production filter or optimize thresholds.

## Frozen definitions
- Data: Supabase `public.daily_bars`.
- OLS: exact current 26-price, nominal HL10 implementation from the full-market research engine.
- Cluster90: monthly point-in-time liquidity-first 120d return-correlation dedup, rho>=0.90 treated as duplicate.
- Fixed15: frozen original 15-asset research universe.
- Healthy new leader: a new non-cash Top1 entry with both:
  - `ScoreDelta_t = Score_t - Score_{t-1} > 0`;
  - valid Volume Profile history and `ResistancePct252 < 75%`.
- Volume Profile definition is unchanged from frozen V1: prior 120 days amount uniformly distributed through each day's [low,high], `UpperShare` over [P,1.05P]/[0.95P,1.05P], point-in-time percentile versus prior 252 observations, minimum 60 observations.
- Primary period: 2021-01-01 through 2024-12-31.
- Posterior period: 2025-01-01 through latest available date.
- No threshold search.

## Attribution tests

### A. Identity effect inside Cluster90
Split healthy Cluster90 new-Top1 entries into:
1. symbol belongs to fixed15;
2. symbol is outside fixed15.
Compare event-weighted and symbol-equal-weighted forward 5/10/20d returns, raw OLS remaining life, MA10 remaining life, signal tenure, and win rates.

### B. Small-universe vs large-universe selector
Compare healthy new-Top1 entries generated by fixed15 itself versus healthy new-Top1 entries generated by Cluster90, using the same state metrics and forward labels.

### C. Same-asset context
Restrict to fixed15 symbols only. Compare their healthy Top1 entries when selected by the fixed15 selector versus when selected by Cluster90. This isolates whether the same assets deteriorate merely because they are chosen as the extreme maximum in a larger cross-section.

### D. Paired disagreement dates
On dates when Cluster90 has a healthy new Top1 outside fixed15 and the fixed15 selector holds/selects another eligible risk asset, compare their forward 5/10/20d returns on the same signal date.

### E. Historical-product control
Within Cluster90 outside-fixed15 healthy leaders, separately report symbols that already had at least 252 daily bars before 2021-01-01. This checks whether results are driven mainly by later ETF product expansion.

### F. Cross-sectional environment
For healthy Cluster90 new leaders, report candidate count, Top1/Top2 score ratio, Top1 score relative to candidate median, raw episode age, and remaining trend life. Use natural within-period medians for descriptive splits only; do not turn them into trading thresholds.

## Interpretation order
1. If fixed15 identity dominates within Cluster90, the curated asset set carries genuine prior information / regime exposure not captured by OLS health.
2. If the same fixed15 assets degrade when selected by Cluster90, large-cross-section extreme-value selection is a major mechanism.
3. If paired Cluster90 outsiders consistently beat fixed15 alternatives but portfolio still loses, execution/turnover is the main issue.
4. If outsiders themselves have near-zero/negative forward returns, opportunity-selection quality is the main issue.

No strategy change will be made from this diagnostic alone.
